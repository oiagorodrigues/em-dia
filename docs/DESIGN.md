# Design: "em dia"

The product is called **em dia** (lowercase, two words). The name has two readings: payments kept _em dia_ (up to date) and the _diária_ (a day's work). The wordmark is "em dia" in Bricolage Grotesque 800 followed by a round stamp-red dot drawn as a circle (0.24em, sitting on the baseline), never a typed period. The app icon is a geometric lowercase "e" (a ring with a crossbar and a wedge cut, in `--sheet` on `--ink`) with the same red dot; source in `apps/web/public/logo.svg`, PNGs generated by `pwa-assets-generator`. "Bater o ponto" stays as the name of the clock-in action. The visual language is a reinterpretation of the Brazilian _cartão de ponto_ (mechanical time card) and the _caderneta_ (small notebook where families write down the diarista's days and payments), drawn with two inks on paper: **ballpoint blue** for what people write and **stamp red** for what the clock stamps.

Version 2 (October 2026) keeps the time-card identity and makes it warmer and more alive: pastel tints, a colored sidebar, roomier cards and tables that use the whole screen on desktop. The people using it are families, donas de casa, governantas and the workers themselves. It should feel friendly and calm, never like accounting software.

Still banned: purple/indigo as a brand color, gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, emoji, Inter/Roboto/system-ui as the display face, hero illustrations, grids of identical icon-in-the-corner cards.

Dark ("noite"): `--bg #111318`, `--surface #191C23`, `--surface-2 #20242D`, `--sidebar #15251E`, `--sidebar-ink #CDEBDC`, `--sidebar-active #1F3329`, `--ink #EEEDE8`, `--ink-2 #A5A9B4`, `--ink-3 #676C78`, `--rule #2A2F39`, `--blue #8FA0FF`, `--blue-wash #1E2544`, `--red #FF7A66`, `--red-wash #3A1F1B`, `--red-text #FF9A8A`, `--green #4FCB94`, `--green-wash #16301F`; tints become dark washes with light ink (mint `#16302A`/`#8FE0BC`, peach `#3A2419`/`#FFB899`, butter `#332A10`/`#F2D27A`, sky `#1A2740`/`#A9C5FF`, rose `#3A1C27`/`#F5A8C2`). Theme follows `prefers-color-scheme` with a manual override (`data-theme` on `<html>`).

Radius: `--r-1: 8px` (inputs, buttons, chips), `--r-2: 14px` (cards, tables, sheets), `--r-3: 20px` (KPI cards, hero). Avatars and status dots are round.

Elevation: one soft shadow, `--shadow-1: 0 1px 2px rgb(27 29 36 / 0.06), 0 6px 20px rgb(27 29 36 / 0.05)`, for cards and tables on `--bg`; dialogs use `--shadow-2` (stronger). The primary button and the clock button keep the hard offset shadow `2px 2px 0 var(--ink)` that collapses on `:active`: the "physical press" stays a brand signature.

Spacing: 4px base scale `--s-1 … --s-10` (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 56, 72).

## Type

Self-hosted via `@fontsource` (works offline in the PWA):

- **Bricolage Grotesque Variable** for everything textual. Display sizes use `font-variation-settings: "opsz" 96` and weight 700 with tight tracking (`-0.03em`). Body 16px/1.5, weight 400.
- **Martian Mono Variable** for every number, time, date and money value, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Width axis condensed (`"wdth" 87.5`) for tables. This is the "stamped by the clock" voice.

Scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 / 40 / 64 / 96. The dashboard hero number (hours this month, e.g. `86h40`) is Martian Mono at 64–96px.

Labels above values are 12px uppercase Bricolage, `letter-spacing: 0.08em`, `--ink-2`, like printed form field labels.

## Signature elements

These survive the v2 refresh, recolored with the new tokens (paper → `--surface`, stamp red → coral).

1. **The time card (cartão).** The month view is a vertical card on `--sheet` with a 2px top notch row (a row of small semicircle cut-outs made with a radial-gradient mask) like a perforated card. Each day is a row: weekday letter + day number on the left, then entrada / saída / pausa / total columns in Martian Mono. Values entered by people are in `--blue`. Absences get a rotated (-4deg) red outlined stamp "FALTA" / "FOLGA" / "FERIADO" / "JUSTIF." in uppercase mono, slightly translucent like real ink. Expected days (`MonthSummary.expectedDates`, from the contract schedule) that passed with nothing recorded show a dotted placeholder "— registrar"; future expected days show a quiet dotted "previsto". `per_month` and `on_demand` schedules have no expected dates, so no placeholders: `per_month` shows "visitas: 1 de 2" under the month name instead. Today's row has a 3px blue left bar. Unexpected weekends dimmed. When expected days are left unrecorded the employer gets "marcar como trabalhados", which fills them in one go (times prefilled from the contract) after a confirmation sheet. Unconfirmed entries show a small hollow circle; confirmed ones a filled green dot.
2. **The clock (bater ponto).** On the worker's home and on the employer's contract page: a large circular button (min 168px) with an analog-clock tick ring drawn in SVG, current time in Martian Mono in the centre, label "bater entrada" / "bater saída". Pressing it plays a short stamp animation (scale 0.96 → 1 + a red ring ink-splash that fades), then the new time appears in the card row with a typewriter reveal.
3. **Receipts as stubs.** Payments are listed as receipt stubs: a sheet with a dashed perforation line and two half-circle notches on the sides separating the main part (month, amount large in mono, method, date) from the stub (receipt thumbnail or "sem comprovante", acknowledgement state "recebido ✓" in green once the worker taps "recebi").
4. **Ledger totals.** Month totals are a two-column ledger with dotted leaders between label and value (`Horas ........ 86h40`), a double rule above the final "a receber" line, like a hand-written account.
5. **Month switcher.** Big lowercase month name ("outubro") with the year small in mono, flanked by ← → arrow buttons; swipe left/right on touch devices.

## Layout

Breakpoints: `sm` < 640, `md` 640–959, `lg` ≥ 960 (sidebar appears), `xl` ≥ 1280, `2xl` ≥ 1600.

Content uses the screen. Max content width is 1440px with a fluid gutter `clamp(16px, 3vw, 48px)`; nothing sits in a narrow 720px column on desktop anymore. Forms are the only exception (a readable column of ~640px, with a sticky side panel on `lg`). Pages lay out on a 12-column grid with 24px gaps (`lg`) and stack to one column below `lg`.

### Navigation

- `lg`+: fixed left sidebar 264px on `--sidebar`, wordmark on top, nav items (icon + label, 44px tall, active item on `--sidebar-active` with a 3px blue bar), and at the bottom a user card (avatar, name, role) with a **sair** button. Employer items: Início, Equipe, Cartão, Pagamentos, Perfil. Worker items: Início, Cartão, Pagamentos, Perfil.
- below `lg`: bottom bar with the same items (employer gets 5), safe-area aware; **sair** lives in Perfil and in the account menu of the top bar.

### Tables and lists (system-wide rule)

Every collection is a **table on `md`+ and a list of cards on `sm`**, rendered by one `DataTable` component from the same column definition. Tables have:

- a toolbar: search box (matches name and text columns, accent-insensitive), filter chips or selects, a result count, and the page's primary action on the right;
- sortable headers (`aria-sort`, click or Enter), default sort per table;
- row hover on `--surface-2`, whole row clickable when it has a detail page (Enter opens it), inline quick-action icon buttons at the end of the row (with tooltips and `aria-label`), plus a `⋯` menu for the rest;
- money, hours and dates right-aligned in Martian Mono; status as pastel chips;
- empty state and "nenhum resultado para …" state with a clear-filters button;
- search, filters and sort mirrored in the URL query, so a link or reload keeps them.

Tables: team (dashboard + Equipe), payments (per person and across the team), extras, activity, the month board, the time card.

### Screens

- **Início (employer)**: header with greeting, month switcher and the actions "adicionar profissional" and "registrar pagamento". Row of 4 KPI mini cards on pastel tints: horas no mês (mint), a pagar (peach), pago no mês (sky), pendências (butter: dias sem registro + aguardando confirmação + pagamentos sem "recebi"). Each KPI has a small caption with context (e.g. "3 pessoas", "de R$ 5.930,00"). Below on `xl`: team table (8 cols) and a right column (4 cols) with **hoje** (who is expected today, their status and a quick "bater entrada") and **pendências** (actionable items that link to the right place). On smaller screens these stack; on `sm` the team is a card list. Team table columns: profissional (avatar, name, função), frequência, hoje, horas, dias (x/y or visitas), a pagar, status; row actions: bater ponto, registrar pagamento, ver cartão, `⋯` (copiar convite, editar, remover).
- **Equipe** (`/equipe`): the team management module. Status tabs with counts (ativos, convites pendentes, encerrados, removidos), search, filters (função, forma de pagamento), sort; the same table with more columns (forma de pagamento + valor, contato). Removed people show "restaurar". Primary action "adicionar profissional".
- **Profissional** (`/equipe/:id`, old `/vinculos/:id` redirects): header with avatar, name, função · frequência, status chip, contact actions (WhatsApp, ligar, copiar Pix) and actions (registrar pagamento, editar, `⋯` remover). Tabs: Visão geral, Cartão, Pagamentos, Extras, Histórico, Ajustes. **Visão geral**: KPI mini cards for the month, custo estimado vs. realizado, combinado (frequência, horários, valor, desconto de faltas), contato, últimos registros, últimos pagamentos. **Cartão** on `lg`: time card table (8 cols) + sticky aside (4 cols) with the clock, the month ledger and "marcar como trabalhados".
- **Cartão** (employer, `/cartao`): the **quadro do mês**, a board with one row per person and one column per day of the month. Cells are small rounded squares: worked = mint with hours on hover/focus, falta = coral, justificada/folga/feriado = butter, previsto sem registro = dashed outline, today highlighted. Row totals (horas, dias, a pagar) at the end, click a row to open that person's card. Below `md` it becomes the list of people with a mini week strip.
- **Pagamentos** (employer, `/pagamentos`): all payments across the team in one table (pessoa, referência, pago em, valor, forma, comprovante, recebido) with filters (pessoa, ano/mês, forma, recebido) and totals; worker sees their own. The receipt stub stays the card form on `sm`.
- **Adicionar profissional** (`/equipe/novo`): form column + sticky **resumo** panel on `lg` (below the form on smaller screens). Sections: quem é (nome, função), contato opcional (e-mail, telefone: "se você preencher, a gente manda o link de cadastro"), pagamento (forma, valor, descontar faltas), frequência, horários, início. The resumo shows the frequency in words and the **custo estimado** from `estimateMonthCost`: "R$ 1.620,00 em outubro · 9 diárias × R$ 180,00", for hourly "9 dias × 8h × R$ 25,00", for monthly the salary, plus "média de R$ 1.580,00 por mês" from `estimateAverageMonthlyCost`; when it can't be priced it says why ("informe os horários para estimar"). After saving: a success view with the invite (code, link, "e-mail enviado para …" when sent, a WhatsApp button when a phone is known, copiar link).
- **Remover**: logical removal from the row menu or the person's page, confirmed in a dialog ("O histórico fica guardado e você pode restaurar em Equipe → removidos."). Undo toast for a few seconds.
- **Início (worker)** on `lg`: two columns, the clock and today on the left, the month ledger, KPI cards and last payment on the right.
- Auth screens, day editor, profile and join code keep their v1 behavior with the v2 tokens.

## Motion

Short and physical: 120–180ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`. Stamp animation 260ms. Bottom sheet slides up 220ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable all but opacity).

## Writing

pt-BR, informal, short. Lowercase headings in the app chrome ("cartão de outubro"). Numbers always in mono. Money as `R$ 1.234,56`. Durations as `86h40`. Never "Usuário"; say "você".

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast for text on both themes. Every control reachable by keyboard, visible focus ring `2px solid var(--blue)` with 2px offset. Stamps have `aria-label` with the full status. Bottom sheets are `<dialog>` with focus trap and Esc to close. Touch targets ≥ 44px. `lang="pt-BR"`.
