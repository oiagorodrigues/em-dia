# Design: "em dia"

The product is called **em dia** (lowercase, two words). The name has two readings: payments kept _em dia_ (up to date) and the _diária_ (a day's work). The wordmark is "em dia" in Bricolage Grotesque 800 followed by a round stamp-red dot drawn as a circle (0.24em, sitting on the baseline), never a typed period. The app icon is a geometric lowercase "e" (a ring with a crossbar and a wedge cut, in `--surface` on `--ink`) with the same red dot; source in `apps/web/public/logo.svg`, PNGs generated by `pwa-assets-generator`. "Bater o ponto" stays as the name of the clock-in action. The visual language is a reinterpretation of the Brazilian _cartão de ponto_ (mechanical time card) and the _caderneta_ (small notebook where families write down the diarista's days and payments), drawn on off-white paper with a green that says "em dia" (up to date) and a coral stamp for what is missing.

Version 3 (October 2026) calms v2 down. Three colors that belong together on an off-white page, soft elevation instead of hard "arcade" shadows, motion that makes the app feel alive, and native-feeling controls. Color always means something; it is never decoration.

Still banned: purple/indigo, gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, emoji, Inter/Roboto/system-ui as the display face, hero illustrations, hard offset shadows, a different color per card "to look lively".

Form values, table text and numbers are `--ink`. Color on a value only when the color carries meaning: money still owed (amber when overdue), negative amounts and deductions (coral), paid/received (green). Avatars use `--brand-wash` with `--brand-ink` initials for everyone. KPI cards are white surfaces with a small icon chip in the color of their meaning (brand for hours/paid, amber for pending, coral only when something is wrong).

Radius: `--r-1: 8px`, `--r-2: 12px`, `--r-3: 16px`. Elevation: `--shadow-1: 0 1px 2px rgb(16 24 20 / 0.05), 0 2px 8px rgb(16 24 20 / 0.04)` at rest, `--shadow-2: 0 4px 12px rgb(16 24 20 / 0.08), 0 12px 32px rgb(16 24 20 / 0.06)` for hover lift, popovers and dialogs. No hard offset shadows anywhere (buttons and the clock included).

## Motion

Make it feel alive, never slow. Durations 120–220ms, easing `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)` for entering and `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)` for leaving.

- Route change: content fades in and rises 8px; tabs inside a page cross-fade. The active tab underline and the active nav indicator slide between items.
- Lists and table rows fade in with a short stagger (20ms per item, capped at 8 items) on first load and when filters change; removed rows collapse and fade.
- Hover: rows tint to `--surface-2`; cards lift 1px with `--shadow-2`; icon buttons get a tinted round background; links underline in.
- Press: buttons scale to 0.98.
- Dialogs fade and scale from 0.98; bottom drawers slide up; menus and popovers fade and slide 4px from their trigger; toasts slide in from the bottom.
- KPI numbers count up when the value changes; skeletons shimmer softly.
- The clock stamp keeps its ink-ring animation in brand green.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: opacity only.

## Grid and layout

Tailwind CSS v4 (`@tailwindcss/vite`) is the layout layer: a 12-column grid (`grid grid-cols-12 gap-6`, `col-span-*` with `md:`/`lg:`/`xl:` variants), spacing and flex utilities. Tokens are exposed to Tailwind through `@theme` so utilities use the same CSS variables (`bg-surface`, `text-ink-2`, `border-rule`, `text-brand`…). Components keep scoped CSS for their own internals; pages compose layout with utilities. No raw hex values in either.

## Controls

- **Select**: a custom select (button + listbox popover, ARIA listbox pattern, type-ahead, keyboard) on fine pointers; on touch (`pointer: coarse`) it opens the native picker, which is the most usable control on phones. The native `<select>` stays in the DOM for forms, autofill and assistive tech. Used for função (always a select, 9 options), filters, and every single-choice group on small screens.
- **Single choice**: segmented controls on `md`+ (como paga, frequência, status) become a Select below `md`.
- **Time**: a custom time field (typed `HH:MM` with mask, plus a popover of 15-minute slots on desktop and a bottom sheet with hour/minute wheels on touch). Never the browser's time popup.
- **Date**: a custom date field (typed `DD/MM/AAAA` plus a month calendar popover; bottom sheet on touch).
- **Stepper**: shows the bare number; the unit is in the label or the summary line, never repeated inside.
- **Tooltips** render in a layer on `<body>` (teleport + positioned with collision handling), so they never clip or widen a table.
- **Filters on mobile**: a "Filtros" button (with the active count) opens a bottom drawer holding the filters, with "limpar" and "ver resultados".
- **KPI cards** lay out with `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr))`: a row when they fit, stacking down to one column when they don't.

## Table actions

- One action: a single inline icon button.
- More than one: up to two of the most frequent actions inline as colored icon buttons, the rest in a `⋯` context menu. Payments rows: "ver" inline; comprovante (ver/anexar/trocar), editar and remover in the menu.
- Inline action colors follow meaning: bater entrada brand green, bater saída amber, registrar pagamento brand, ver neutral. In menus, items are neutral except **remover** in coral.
- Icons: ver = eye; comprovante = paperclip; editar = pencil; remover = trash; registrar pagamento = wallet-plus; bater entrada = arrow entering a door; bater saída = arrow leaving a door. Never a chevron/arrow for "open".

## Navigation

"voltar" goes to the screen the person came from when it is inside the app (router history `state.back`), and to the module's list only when the page was opened directly. Saving a form returns to where the form was opened from.

## Type

Self-hosted via `@fontsource` (works offline in the PWA):

- **Bricolage Grotesque Variable** for everything textual. Display sizes use `font-variation-settings: "opsz" 96` and weight 700 with tight tracking (`-0.03em`). Body 16px/1.5, weight 400.
- **Martian Mono Variable** for every number, time, date and money value, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Width axis condensed (`"wdth" 87.5`) for tables. This is the "stamped by the clock" voice.

Scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 / 40 / 64 / 96. The dashboard hero number (hours this month, e.g. `86h40`) is Martian Mono at 64–96px.

Labels above values are 12px uppercase Bricolage, `letter-spacing: 0.08em`, `--ink-2`, like printed form field labels.

## Signature elements

These survive v3, recolored with its three colors: worked/positive in brand green, stamps and absences in coral, pending in amber. People-typed values are `--ink` (no more ballpoint blue).

1. **The time card (cartão).** The month view is a vertical card on `--surface` with a 2px top notch row (a row of small semicircle cut-outs made with a radial-gradient mask) like a perforated card. Each day is a row: weekday letter + day number on the left, then entrada / saída / pausa / total columns in Martian Mono. Absences get a rotated (-4deg) red outlined stamp "FALTA" / "FOLGA" / "FERIADO" / "JUSTIF." in uppercase mono, slightly translucent like real ink. Expected days (`MonthSummary.expectedDates`, from the contract schedule) that passed with nothing recorded show a dotted placeholder "— registrar"; future expected days show a quiet dotted "previsto". `per_month` and `on_demand` schedules have no expected dates, so no placeholders: `per_month` shows "visitas: 1 de 2" under the month name instead. Today's row has a 3px brand-green left bar. Unexpected weekends dimmed. When expected days are left unrecorded the employer gets "marcar como trabalhados", which fills them in one go (times prefilled from the contract) after a confirmation sheet. Unconfirmed entries show a small hollow circle; confirmed ones a filled green dot.
2. **The clock (bater ponto).** On the worker's home and on the employer's contract page: a large circular button (min 168px) with an analog-clock tick ring drawn in SVG, current time in Martian Mono in the centre, label "bater entrada" / "bater saída" with distinct icons (arrow entering / leaving a door). Pressing it plays a short stamp animation (scale 0.96 → 1 + a brand-green ring ink-splash that fades), then the new time appears in the card row with a typewriter reveal.
3. **Receipts as stubs.** Payments are listed as receipt stubs: a sheet with a dashed perforation line and two half-circle notches on the sides separating the main part (month, amount large in mono, method, date) from the stub (receipt thumbnail or "sem comprovante", acknowledgement state "recebido ✓" in green once the worker taps "recebi").
4. **Ledger totals.** Month totals are a two-column ledger with dotted leaders between label and value (`Horas ........ 86h40`), a double rule above the final "a receber" line, like a hand-written account.
5. **Month switcher.** Big lowercase month name ("outubro") with the year small in mono, flanked by ← → arrow buttons; swipe left/right on touch devices.

## Layout

Breakpoints: `sm` < 640, `md` 640–959, `lg` ≥ 960 (sidebar appears), `xl` ≥ 1280, `2xl` ≥ 1600.

Content uses the screen. Max content width is 1440px with a fluid gutter `clamp(16px, 3vw, 48px)`; nothing sits in a narrow 720px column on desktop anymore. Forms are the only exception (a readable column of ~640px, with a sticky side panel on `lg`). Pages lay out on a 12-column grid with 24px gaps (`lg`) and stack to one column below `lg`.

### Navigation

- `lg`+: fixed left sidebar 264px on `--sidebar`, wordmark on top, nav items (icon + label, 44px tall, active item on `--sidebar-active` with a sliding brand-green indicator), and at the bottom a user card (avatar, name, role) with a **sair** button. Employer items: Início, Equipe, Cartão, Pagamentos, Perfil. Worker items: Início, Cartão, Pagamentos, Perfil.
- below `lg`: bottom bar with the same items (employer gets 5), safe-area aware; **sair** lives in Perfil and in the account menu of the top bar.

### Tables and lists (system-wide rule)

Every collection is a **table on `md`+ and a list of cards on `sm`**, rendered by one `DataTable` component from the same column definition. Tables have:

- a toolbar: search box (matches name and text columns, accent-insensitive), filter chips or selects, a result count, and the page's primary action on the right;
- sortable headers (`aria-sort`, click or Enter), default sort per table;
- row hover on `--surface-2`, whole row clickable when it has a detail page (Enter opens it), inline quick-action icon buttons at the end of the row (with tooltips and `aria-label`), plus a `⋯` menu for the rest;
- money, hours and dates right-aligned in Martian Mono; status as chips in the color of their meaning (green, amber, coral, neutral);
- empty state and "nenhum resultado para …" state with a clear-filters button;
- search, filters and sort mirrored in the URL query, so a link or reload keeps them.

Tables: team (dashboard + Equipe), payments (per person and across the team), extras, activity, the month board, the time card.

## Writing

Titles and headings start with a capital letter (sentence case: "Adicionar profissional", "Cartão de outubro"). Say plainly what will happen: "Salvar profissional" (not "cadastrar e gerar convite"), "Convite para o app" when talking about the invite. Avoid symbols people may not read ("~4 dias" → "4 dias previstos").

pt-BR, informal, short. Numbers always in mono. Money as `R$ 1.234,56`. Durations as `86h40`. Never "Usuário"; say "você".

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast for text on both themes. Every control reachable by keyboard, visible focus ring `2px solid var(--brand)` with 2px offset. Stamps have `aria-label` with the full status. Bottom sheets are `<dialog>` with focus trap and Esc to close. Touch targets ≥ 44px. `lang="pt-BR"`.
