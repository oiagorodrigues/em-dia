# Product sense

## Users

- Employer families: a household that hires a diarista, babysitter, cleaner, caregiver or driver, often informally and often more than one person. They are not accountants. They want to know what they owe and have proof they paid.
- Domestic workers: paid by the hour, day or month, often working for several households. They want a record of the days they came and a receipt for what they were paid. Many use a cheap Android phone with a flaky connection.

## Real problems

- "Which days did she come this month?" Families forget, and the answer is a guess on payday.
- Disputes about hours: each side remembers differently and there is no shared record.
- Proof of payment: Pix and cash payments leave nothing the worker can show later, and the employer cannot show they paid.
- Pix: the employer needs the worker's key at hand when paying.
- Extras and advances (vale, transporte, desconto) are forgotten or argued over at month end.

## Schedules in real households

The agreement between family and worker differs a lot, so the app models the agreement instead of assuming "weekdays":

- Diarista 2x por semana (say terça e sexta): paid per day, expected on fixed weekdays. The family forgets which days she came; the card shows the gaps.
- Faxina 1x por mês: paid per day, no fixed date. The family only needs to record the day it happened and pay it.
- Mensalista 30 dias (or seg a sex): fixed monthly salary. Hours matter little; absences matter. The family marks all expected days at once and fixes the exceptions, and may choose to deduct unjustified absences as salário ÷ 30.
- Quinzenal: every other week on a weekday. A plain weekday list cannot express it.
- Also common: "todo dia 5" (a fixed date each month) and "quando precisar" (no expectation).

## Principles

- Both sides see the same truth. The employer and the worker read the same time card, adjustments and payments.
- Dual confirmation. An entry is confirmed when the other party confirms it. Editing a confirmed entry clears the confirmation.
- Fast to record. One tap to clock in or out; one sheet to fix a day.
- Plain language, no jargon, no accounting terms. "a receber", "recebi".
- The record is trustworthy: every change is in the activity log with who and when.
- Respect the worker: the app is not surveillance. No location, no photos of the worker, no productivity scoring.
- Money is exact. Totals match what a person would compute by hand.

## Non-goals (MVP)

Payroll taxes (eSocial, INSS, FGTS), payment processing, vacation and 13th salary calculation, native apps, push notifications.
