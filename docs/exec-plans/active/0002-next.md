## Decision log

- 2026-10-06: Plan opened from the leftovers of [0001](../completed/0001-mvp.md).
- 2026-10-06: One TypeScript version for the whole repo, 6.0.3. The web needs vue-tsc, which does not run on TypeScript 7 yet; keeping two versions was confusing. Revisit with TS 7.1 (see tech debt).
- 2026-10-06: Product renamed from "ponto." to "em dia" (payments "em dia" and "diária"); packages are now `@emdia/*`, header value `emdia`, cookie `emdia_rt`. Reason: the new name covers both the payment and the day-rate meanings. "Bater o ponto" stays as the clock-in action.
- 2026-10-06: Contract schedule is a tagged union (`weekly`, `month_days`, `per_month`, `on_demand`) with `everyWeeks`, replacing the weekday list (migration 003). Reason: real arrangements (quinzenal, todo dia 5, 1x por mês, mensalista) cannot be expressed as weekdays.
- 2026-10-06: Unjustified absences deduct salário ÷ 30 only when the contract opts in (`deductAbsences`, monthly pay). Reason: many families do not deduct, and a surprise deduction would break trust.
- 2026-10-06: v2 design system: pastel KPI tints, mint sidebar, tables on desktop and cards on mobile for every collection ([DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md)). Reason: employers manage several people and need to scan and filter, which cards alone do poorly.
- 2026-10-06: "Remover" is logical (`archivedAt`) and reversible; archived contracts are read-only. Reason: pay and hour history is evidence for both sides and must not be lost by a misclick.
- 2026-10-06: WhatsApp invites use a `wa.me` link the employer taps, not server-side sending. Reason: WhatsApp has no free sending API.
- 2026-10-06: Real mail goes through nodemailer over `SMTP_URL`. Reason: works with any provider and needs no vendor SDK.
- 2026-10-06: Design v3 uses three colors (brand green, coral, amber) on neutrals, with color always carrying meaning. Reason: v2's many tints read as decoration.
- 2026-10-06: Tailwind v4 is the layout layer; components keep scoped CSS and tokens, with no raw hex. Reason: a 12-column grid and spacing utilities are faster to compose than hand-written CSS grids.
- 2026-10-06: Agreements are modeled as payments with method `compensation` linked to an agreement. Reason: they reuse totals, acknowledgement and receipts instead of a parallel ledger.
- 2026-10-07: Agreements are removed, not cancelled, from the UI. Removal follows the contract rule: logical (`agreements.archived_at`, migration 0001) and reversible. While removed, the agreement's compensation payments are hidden from every payment list and total, so the paid figures both sides see never include an agreement that is no longer listed; restoring brings them back. The cancel endpoint stays for older clients. Reason: the employer asked to remove agreements made by mistake, and hiding the installments with the agreement keeps the ledger consistent without losing evidence.
- 2026-10-07: The employer home drops the "horas no mês" KPI for "custo do mês" (the month forecast from `DashboardItem.estimate`, shown as "previsto"). Reason: families pay by day or month and budget in money; hours already live in the team table, and pending confirmations and the open balance already have their own cards. Skeletons mirror each screen's layout through shared `components/ui/Skeleton*` pieces.
- 2026-10-06: Account deletion anonymises instead of hard deleting. Reason: the other party's records must stay intact; see [SECURITY](../../SECURITY.md#account-deletion-and-lgpd).
- 2026-10-07: Web reads use TanStack Vue Query behind `useResource` (typed keys in `api/keys.ts`, invalidation after writes, cache cleared on logout). Reason: every screen refetched on each visit and a write on one screen left the others stale; a keyed cache shows the last data at once and refreshes what a write touched. See [FRONTEND](../../FRONTEND.md#data-fetching).
- 2026-10-07: The sidebar collapses to icons (76px) with the "e" mark and right-side tooltips; the choice is a per-browser `localStorage` preference, not account data. Reason: on laptop screens the board and tables need the width, and a UI preference doesn't need a server round trip.
- 2026-10-07: Long page-header actions are icon-only with a tooltip on `md`+ with a mouse, and keep their text on touch screens. Reason: the headers were crowded by two long buttons, and touch has no hover to reveal a tooltip.
- 2026-10-07: Tooltips open after 500ms of hover (instant on keyboard focus and right after another tooltip). Reason: shorter delays flashed labels while moving across row actions.
- 2026-10-07: One icon per action in `ACTION_ICON`; remover is always `trash` (it was `archive` on the person's page) and registrar pagamento always `wallet-plus` (it was `coins` or `plus` in places). Reason: the same action looked different on each screen.
- 2026-10-07: All standalone UI text is sentence case, chips and buttons included. Reason: lowercase chips and buttons next to capitalized headings read as inconsistent.
