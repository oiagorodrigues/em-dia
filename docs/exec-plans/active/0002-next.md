## Decision log

- 2026-10-06: Plan opened from the leftovers of [0001](../completed/0001-mvp.md).
- 2026-10-06: One TypeScript version for the whole repo, 6.0.3. The web needs vue-tsc, which does not run on TypeScript 7 yet; keeping two versions was confusing. Revisit with TS 7.1 (see tech debt).
- 2026-10-06: Product renamed from "ponto." to "em dia" (payments "em dia" and "diária"); packages are now `@emdia/*`, header value `emdia`, cookie `emdia_rt`. Reason: the new name covers both the payment and the day-rate meanings. "Bater o ponto" stays as the clock-in action.
- 2026-10-06: Contract schedule is a tagged union (`weekly`, `month_days`, `per_month`, `on_demand`) with `everyWeeks`, replacing the weekday list (migration 003). Reason: real arrangements (quinzenal, todo dia 5, 1x por mês, mensalista) cannot be expressed as weekdays.
- 2026-10-06: Unjustified absences deduct salário ÷ 30 only when the contract opts in (`deductAbsences`, monthly pay). Reason: many families do not deduct, and a surprise deduction would break trust.
- 2026-10-06: v2 design system: pastel KPI tints, mint sidebar, tables on desktop and cards on mobile for every collection ([DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md)). Reason: employers manage several people and need to scan and filter, which cards alone do poorly.
- 2026-10-06: "Remover" is logical (`archivedAt`) and reversible; archived contracts are read-only. Reason: pay and hour history is evidence for both sides and must not be lost by a misclick.
- 2026-10-06: WhatsApp invites use a `wa.me` link the employer taps, not server-side sending. Reason: WhatsApp has no free sending API.
- 2026-10-06: Real mail goes through nodemailer over `SMTP_URL`. Reason: works with any provider and needs no vendor SDK.
- 2026-10-06: Design v3 uses three colors (brand green, coral, amber) on neutrals, with color always carrying meaning. Reason: v2's many tints read as decoration.
- 2026-10-06: Tailwind v4 is the layout layer; components keep scoped CSS and tokens, with no raw hex. Reason: a 12-column grid and spacing utilities are faster to compose than hand-written CSS grids.
- 2026-10-06: Agreements are modeled as payments with method `compensation` linked to an agreement. Reason: they reuse totals, acknowledgement and receipts instead of a parallel ledger.
- 2026-10-08: The sidebar toggle moves to the top, icon-only beside the wordmark and outside its link, with ⌘B / Ctrl+B. Reason: the product owner asked for it at the top instead of as a nav item; a control next to what it controls, in the same spot open or collapsed, is the common pattern (shadcn/ui sidebar trigger, GitLab's navigation sidebar), and keeping it out of the logo link avoids hitting "início" by mistake.
- 2026-10-08: `Contract.workerEmail` exposes the linked worker's account e-mail to the employer, next to the phone and Pix key already shown. Reason: the product owner wants the employer to see the contact the worker registered; the employer already knows who they hired, and the worker's account e-mail is the reliable way to reach them once the typed contact is out of date.
- 2026-10-08: The Ajustes tab becomes an edit sheet opened from "Editar"; the convite moves to Visão geral and "encerrar vínculo" to the `⋯` menu. Reason: the product owner wants editing in a modal; a tab for a form used once in a while took a slot the person's history uses daily.
- 2026-10-08: "Por plantão" is a pay type (rate per 24h, migration `0005_shift_pay_night_bonus`), and an entry may end on a later date (`checkOutDate`, up to 7 days). Reason: the product owner chose "diária de 24h" for workers who stay from Friday to Monday. Rejected: one "dia todo" entry per day, which splits one stay into several records and confirmations, and paying a plantão as diárias over the agreed times, which ties the price to times a 24h stay doesn't have.
- 2026-10-08: The adicional noturno (20% on 22:00–05:00) is a per-contract switch, on for new contracts and off for existing ones, without the reduced night hour. Reason: the law sets the 20% minimum for domestic workers (LC 150 art. 14) and the product owner wants it on by default but switchable "em acordo com o profissional"; turning it on for old contracts would change months already closed. The 52min30s hour is left out to keep the number checkable by hand.
- 2026-10-08: A worker who already has an account joins another household with the invite code from the app ("Entrar em outro vínculo") or by signing in from the invite link, which now keeps the code through login. Reason: the API already supported several contracts per worker; only the way in was missing.
- 2026-10-07: Agreements are removed, not cancelled, from the UI. Removal follows the contract rule: logical (`agreements.archived_at`, migration 0001) and reversible. While removed, the agreement's compensation payments are hidden from every payment list and total, so the paid figures both sides see never include an agreement that is no longer listed; restoring brings them back. The cancel endpoint stays for older clients. Reason: the employer asked to remove agreements made by mistake, and hiding the installments with the agreement keeps the ledger consistent without losing evidence.
- 2026-10-07: The employer home drops the "horas no mês" KPI for "custo do mês" (the month forecast from `DashboardItem.estimate`, shown as "previsto"). Reason: families pay by day or month and budget in money; hours already live in the team table, and pending confirmations and the open balance already have their own cards. Skeletons mirror each screen's layout through shared `components/ui/Skeleton*` pieces.
- 2026-10-06: Account deletion anonymises instead of hard deleting. Reason: the other party's records must stay intact; see [SECURITY](../../SECURITY.md#account-deletion-and-lgpd).
- 2026-10-07: The employer can close a payment without the worker ("marcar como concluído", `POST /payments/:pid/confirm`, permission `payment:confirm`). It reuses `acknowledgedAt` and adds `acknowledgedBy` + `acknowledgedByRole`, so every reader of "is it settled" keeps working and the UI can say "recebido" (worker) or "concluído" (employer). Migration `0002_payments_confirm_extra` backfills existing acknowledgements as the worker's. Reason: many workers never open the app, and the employer still needs to close the month.
- 2026-10-07: Payments are editable with `PATCH /payments/:pid`; editing the month, date, amount, method or agreement clears the confirmation. Reason: a "recebi" vouches for specific values and must not silently cover new ones.
- 2026-10-07: `Payment.isExtra` marks money paid on top of the month's pay. The pay math counts it both as an extra (due) and as paid, so the balance is unchanged, and the Extras tab lists it next to adjustments. Reason: paying a one-off extra must not look like an advance on the salary. Open question: an employer who also adds an "extra" adjustment for the same thing counts it twice.
- 2026-10-07: `DataTable` keeps row actions visible by dropping low-priority columns (and tightening padding) when the table overflows, instead of a sticky actions column. Reason: sticky columns cover content and hide the overflow problem rather than solve it.
- 2026-10-07: Web reads use TanStack Vue Query behind `useResource` (typed keys in `api/keys.ts`, invalidation after writes, cache cleared on logout). Reason: every screen refetched on each visit and a write on one screen left the others stale; a keyed cache shows the last data at once and refreshes what a write touched. See [FRONTEND](../../FRONTEND.md#data-fetching).
- 2026-10-07: A payment made from the day editor refers to the worked day's month, whatever the payment date. "Novo pagamento" keeps today's month as the editable default. Reason: paying September's diárias in early October must settle September, not October.
- 2026-10-07: On the Cartão board a day opens the day editor in place (with the optional payment section) instead of navigating to the person's page; the row still opens the person. Reason: recording and paying a day are the most frequent board actions.
- 2026-10-07: Every-N-weeks schedules count from the week of the first scheduled weekday on or after the start, not the start date's own week. Reason: a quinzenal Friday starting on a Saturday skipped its first Friday, so estimates and expected days were a week off.
- 2026-10-07: The sidebar collapses to icons (76px) with the "e" mark and right-side tooltips; the choice is a per-browser `localStorage` preference, not account data. Reason: on laptop screens the board and tables need the width, and a UI preference doesn't need a server round trip.
- 2026-10-07: Long page-header actions are icon-only with a tooltip on `md`+ with a mouse, and keep their text on touch screens. Reason: the headers were crowded by two long buttons, and touch has no hover to reveal a tooltip.
- 2026-10-07: Tooltips open after 500ms of hover (instant on keyboard focus and right after another tooltip). Reason: shorter delays flashed labels while moving across row actions.
- 2026-10-07: One icon per action in `ACTION_ICON`; remover is always `trash` (it was `archive` on the person's page) and registrar pagamento always `wallet-plus` (it was `coins` or `plus` in places). Reason: the same action looked different on each screen.
- 2026-10-07: All standalone UI text is sentence case, chips and buttons included. Reason: lowercase chips and buttons next to capitalized headings read as inconsistent.
- 2026-10-07: Pinia Colada (`@pinia/colada` 1.4.7, retries through `@pinia/colada-plugin-retry`) replaces TanStack Vue Query behind `useResource`/`useInvalidate`; Vue is pinned to 3.5.41. Reason: Pinia is already in use, the cache lives in a Pinia store with its devtools, and the bundle is smaller. The Vue 3.6 release candidate is dropped because Colada peers on `vue ^3.5.41`; this reverts the 3.6 choice in [0001](../completed/0001-mvp.md) (no code used a 3.6-only API).
- 2026-10-07: Extras move into Pagamentos. Every entry is a `Payment` with a `kind` (`pay`, `bonus` gratificação, `transport` vale-transporte, `advance` adiantamento, `deduction` desconto); the Extras tab, the `adjustments` table and API and `Payment.isExtra` are removed. Gratificação and vale-transporte count as due and as paid (what `isExtra` did); a desconto only lowers what is due and has `method: null`, no receipt and no recebi. Reason: the product owner wants one list of everything that changes the month, and the open question of the `isExtra` entry (an extra counted twice) goes away when there is one place to record it.
- 2026-10-07: An adiantamento is paid down with the compensation mechanism agreements use, not a new ledger and not an agreement row. The advance is a payment of kind `advance` that changes nothing when handed; each abated day is a `pay` payment with method `compensation` and `advanceId` (self-reference in `payments`), and `remainingCents` is derived from them like an agreement's. Reason: an advance is a worker debt paid with work, so compensation already has the right meaning (counts as paid, goes through recebi), while an agreement row would add a second object to keep in sync, a worker acceptance step that makes no sense for money already received, and removal rules (hide the installments) that contradict "block removal while days are abated". Abating blocks amounts over what remains (API 400, UI message) instead of capping silently; the day editor prefills the capped value and says the rest stays to be paid.
- 2026-10-07: Migration `0003_payment_kinds` moves history without changing any month's due or paid amounts. `is_extra` payments become `bonus`. Each adjustment becomes a payment row with `method` null: bonus and transport keep their kind and count in due only (their money went out inside another payment, so counting them as paid would double count it); deductions become descontos; old advances also become descontos, noted "Adiantamento (vale)", because they lowered the amount due at once. Rejected: migrating old advances as fully abated adiantamentos, which keeps the balance but moves both due and paid. The API never creates a methodless gratificação; an edit may keep one. Audit rows of `adjustment.*` stay and keep their wording.
- 2026-10-08: Quinzenal is a pay type (`biweekly`, rate per quinzena, migration `0004_biweekly_pay_type` widens the check) and counts as a salary of 2 × rate a month, absences off a thirtieth of that. Rejected: a pay frequency separate from the pay type, which would double the combinations the summary, estimate and forms handle for a case the product owner described as "pagamento quinzenal". Rate equivalents in the resumo are computed (`derivedRates`), not stored, because they change with the schedule and times.
- 2026-10-08: The day editor pays only today's day, dated today; past days are paid in "novo pagamento" (abating from an adiantamento stays open for any past day). The amount for a diária follows the hours in proportion to the agreed times (same break on both sides).
- 2026-10-08: The month's due amount for daily pay is the sum of each worked day in proportion to the agreed times (`workedDayCents`), the same value the day editor fills in. Reason: the product owner wants every pay type on the same basis, hours; hourly already was, and a salary stays fixed. This changes the total of past months whose days ran longer or shorter than the agreed times; it was accepted.
- 2026-10-08: Combined reads (board, payments of the year) fetch their parts through the cache with their own keys, and cached data is kept 30 minutes. Reason: each combined screen refetched data other screens had just loaded, and returning after 5 minutes showed a skeleton. The `agreements` key held two shapes (with and without removed ones) depending on which screen loaded first; every reader now fetches removed ones too.
- 2026-10-07: "Total pago" and the summary's paid figure leave out descontos and adiantamentos (an adiantamento counts as its days are abated), and "aguardando recebi" counts only entries where money changed hands. Reason: an adiantamento counted when handed and again when abated would be paid twice.
