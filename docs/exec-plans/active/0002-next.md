## Decision log

- 2026-10-06: Plan opened from the leftovers of [0001](../completed/0001-mvp.md).
- 2026-10-06: Product renamed from "ponto." to "em dia" (payments "em dia" and "diária"); packages are now `@emdia/*`, header value `emdia`, cookie `emdia_rt`. Reason: the new name covers both the payment and the day-rate meanings. "Bater o ponto" stays as the clock-in action.
- 2026-10-06: Contract schedule is a tagged union (`weekly`, `month_days`, `per_month`, `on_demand`) with `everyWeeks`, replacing the weekday list (migration 003). Reason: real arrangements (quinzenal, todo dia 5, 1x por mês, mensalista) cannot be expressed as weekdays.
- 2026-10-06: Unjustified absences deduct salário ÷ 30 only when the contract opts in (`deductAbsences`, monthly pay). Reason: many families do not deduct, and a surprise deduction would break trust.
- 2026-10-06: v2 design system: pastel KPI tints, mint sidebar, tables on desktop and cards on mobile for every collection ([DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md)). Reason: employers manage several people and need to scan and filter, which cards alone do poorly.
- 2026-10-06: "Remover" is logical (`archivedAt`) and reversible; archived contracts are read-only. Reason: pay and hour history is evidence for both sides and must not be lost by a misclick.
- 2026-10-06: WhatsApp invites use a `wa.me` link the employer taps, not server-side sending. Reason: WhatsApp has no free sending API.
- 2026-10-06: Real mail goes through nodemailer over `SMTP_URL`. Reason: works with any provider and needs no vendor SDK.
- 2026-10-06: Design v3 uses three colors (brand green, coral, amber) on neutrals, with color always carrying meaning. Reason: v2's many tints read as decoration.
- 2026-10-06: Tailwind v4 is the layout layer; components keep scoped CSS and tokens, with no raw hex. Reason: a 12-column grid and spacing utilities are faster to compose than hand-written CSS grids.
- 2026-10-06: Agreements are modeled as payments with method `compensation` linked to an agreement. Reason: they reuse totals, acknowledgement and receipts instead of a parallel ledger.
- 2026-10-06: Account deletion anonymises instead of hard deleting. Reason: the other party's records must stay intact; see [SECURITY](../../SECURITY.md#account-deletion-and-lgpd).

## Status

In progress. Real mail and the v2 team management are in. Next step: backup automation, since it has the lowest grade in [QUALITY_SCORE](../../QUALITY_SCORE.md).
